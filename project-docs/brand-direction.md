## 2. Visual Identity & Editorial Design Language

ManageO rejects the cliché "AI SaaS template" aesthetic. We do not use generic neon purple gradients, floating 3D spheres, random glassmorphism blur without purpose, or generic 3-card feature columns.

Instead, ManageO draws inspiration from **high-fashion editorial magazines**, **architectural monographs**, and **Swiss typography**, grounded by tactile commercial artifacts.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DESIGN LANGUAGE SPECTRUM                                                                         |
|                                                                                                   |
|  AVOID:                                             EMBRACE:                                      |
|  X Generic gradient blobs                           / Asymmetrical, editorial layouts             |

|  X Cookie-cutter 3-card grids                       / Tactile paper artifacts (receipts, tags)    |
|  X Childish cartoon illustrations                   / Ultra-crisp geometric typography            |
|  X Heavy neon drop shadows                          / Clean, razor-thin borders & deep contrasts  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 3. Typographic Architecture

The typography must communicate architectural authority, high precision, and editorial sophistication.

### 3.1. Primary Display Typeface: *Cabinet Grotesk / Plus Jakarta Sans Display*
* **Characteristics:** Geometric grotesque with sharp ink traps, generous counters, and tight negative kerning.
* **Weights:** `Extrabold (800)` and `Bold (700)`.
* **Letter-Spacing:** Tight tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.05em`) to create a compact, punchy editorial headline rhythm.
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Hero Display:** `clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 6.75rem)` (56px to 108px), leading `0.95`.
  * **Section Headings (H2):** `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` (40px to 72px), leading `1.05`.
  * **Subsection Headings (H3):** `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.25rem)` (24px to 36px), leading `1.15`.

### 3.2. Body & Narrative Typeface: *Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans Text*
* **Characteristics:** Engineered for screen legibility, tall x-height, neutral geometric proportions.
* **Weights:** `Regular (400)` and `Medium (500)`.
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Lead Paragraph:** `1.25rem` (20px), leading `1.6`, neutral slate.
  * **Body Standard:** `1.0rem` (16px), leading `1.65`.
  * **Caption / Footnote:** `0.875rem` (14px), leading `1.5`.

### 3.3. Technical & Monospace Typeface: *JetBrains Mono / Space Mono*
* **Characteristics:** Fixed-width tabular numerals, developer/financial precision.
* **Usage:** Section kickers (`■ HIGHLIGHTS`), numerical counters (`01 / 03`), hardware specs (`$899 OR $44/MO`), live receipt totals (`$303.91`), SKU codes, and timestamp logs.
* **Hierarchy:** `0.75rem` - `0.875rem` (12px to 14px), tracking `+0.08em` (uppercase).

## 4. Color Palette & Canvas State Management

ManageO utilizes a **dual-canvas architecture**: transitioning dynamically between crisp, sunlit daylight tones (Storefront clarity) and deep obsidian night tones (System engine power).

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| COLOR SYSTEM MATRIX                                                                              |
|                                                                                                   |
| [LIGHT CANVAS: Daylight Storefront]                 [DARK CANVAS: Operational Engine]            |
| Canvas Background:  #F8F9FA / #FFFFFF               Canvas Background:  #090A0F / #12141A         |
| Surface Cards:      #FFFFFF / #F2F4F7               Surface Cards:      #1A1D26 / #222634         |
| Primary Text:       #0A0D14                         Primary Text:       #FFFFFF                   |
| Secondary Text:     #525866                         Secondary Text:     #94A3B8                   |
| Border Rules:       rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.08)          Border Rules:       rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10) |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BRAND ACCENTS & VERTICAL CODING                                                                  |
| Core Brand Electric Blue:   #2563EB (Hover: #1D4ED8, Subtle BG: #EFF6FF)                         |
| Retail & Shop Emerald:      #059669 (Subtle BG: #ECFDF5, Badge Text: #047857)                    |
| Salon & Beauty Rose Dahlia: #E11D48 (Subtle BG: #FFF1F2, Badge Text: #BE123C)                    |
| Restaurant Saffron Gold:    #D97706 (Subtle BG: #FFFBEB, Badge Text: #B45309)                    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4.1. Light Canvas Specifications
* **Page Base:** `#F8F9FA` (Soft warm bone, preventing sterile harsh white glare).
* **Card Containers:** `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine `1px` border `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
* **Deep Contrast Typography:** `#0A0D14` (Near-black with 2% blue ink tint).

### 4.2. Dark Canvas Specifications
* **Engine Background:** `#090A0F` (Rich obsidian, avoids washed-out greys).
* **Hardware & Terminal Containers:** `#141721` with high-contrast inner borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).
* **Text Contrast:** `#FFFFFF` headlines, `#94A3B8` secondary descriptions.

### 4.3. Vertical Industry Semantic Colors
* **Retail & Shop:** Deep Forest to Vivid Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`) — represents inventory health, cash flow, stock balance.
* **Salon & Beauty:** Velvet Plum to Rose Dahlia (`#E11D48` / `#FB7185`) — represents personal care, luxury, aesthetics, warmth.
* **Restaurant (Coming Soon):** Amber Saffron (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`) — represents culinary craftsmanship, hospitality, energy.

## 5. Compositional Principles & Layout Geometry

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| COMPOSITIONAL LAYOUT PRINCIPLES                                                                   |
|                                                                                                   |
|  [EDITORIAL ASYMMETRY]     [THE FLOATING PILL DOCK]   [TACTILE ARTIFACT COLLAGE]                  |
|  Off-center headlines,      Glassmorphic floating      Thermal receipt with realistic paper rip,   |
|  generous negative space,   pill that pins cleanly     price tags, barcode chits, and genuine      |
|  asymmetric photo crops.    to top on scroll.          lifestyle commercial photography.           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 5.1. The Editorial Asymmetry Principle
Standard SaaS websites repeat identical 3-column cards. ManageO uses **dynamic proportional tension**:
* Section headings anchor hard to the left, balanced by generous negative space or live tactile widgets on the right.
* Split hero compositions: Headlines frame the page like a publication masthead, sandwiching an organic cluster of imagery in the center.

### 5.2. Tactile Physical Artifacts
To ground the software in the tangible world of brick-and-mortar operations, ManageO incorporates bespoke, high-fidelity physical artifacts:
* **The Itemized Thermal Receipt:** Complete with authentic perforated edges, monospaced line items, store header, tax breakdown, and a simulated handwritten signature or message.
* **The Retail Hangtag / Barcode Chit:** Minimalist luxury garment price tag featuring scannable EAN-13 barcode, SKU number, size/color variant, and pricing.
* **The Appointment Time-Block:** Clean dark-mode schedule chit showing client name, requested service, stylist allocation, and time duration.
* **The Table Order Ticket:** Restaurant kitchen chit with seat numbers, course timing, and item modifications ("Medium Rare", "Extra Truffle").

### 5.3. Micro-Kicker Hierarchy (`■`)
Every section on the landing page begins with an authoritative micro-kicker:
* Format: Solid 6x6px square glyph `■` followed by uppercase category name with wide tracking (`tracking-[0.2em]`).
* Examples:
  * `■ CUSTOMIZE`
  * `■ HIGHLIGHTS`
  * `■ EXPLORE ALL FEATURES`
  * `■ UNIFIED ENGINE`
  * `■ HARDWARE ECOSYSTEM`
  * `■ TRANSPARENT PRICING`
  * `■ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS`

## 6. Photography Art Direction

* **Authentic Human Connection:** Capture real merchants, stylists, baristas, and boutique managers in authentic workspaces. Avoid cheesy posed handshakes or stock models staring into webcams.
* **Cinematic Lighting & Depth:** Natural window light, dappled afternoon shadows, shallow depth-of-field, warm wood tones, polished concrete, brushed brass fixtures.
* **Device Integration:** Software interfaces must appear naturally rendered inside real hardware frames (sleek customer-facing displays, iPads with swivel stands, compact handheld mobile terminals) rather than floating as disembodied flat PNGs.
